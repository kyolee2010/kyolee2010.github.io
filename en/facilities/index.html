🔬 Experimental Facilities

We combine in-house test platforms with general-purpose instrumentation to study acoustic materials, liners, complex sound fields, and structural vibration.

Acoustic Material Testing

Conventional Impedance Tubes

Commercial circular tubes and in-house square tubes measure sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and transmission loss for acoustic materials and metamaterial structures.

  • Square-tube sizes: 25, 30, 50, 70, 100, and 180 mm
  • Combined frequency coverage: 20–6400 Hz, depending on tube size and configuration

High-Sound-Pressure Impedance Tube

Coupled high-power sound sources provide large-amplitude excitation to study nonlinear absorption and level-dependent impedance, supporting acoustic liner research under high sound pressure conditions.

High-Temperature Impedance Tube

A heated tube, microphone array, and water-cooled section enable absorption and impedance measurements at elevated temperatures, supporting material characterization under conditions closer to service environments.

Multimodal Waveguides

Microphone arrays resolve higher-order acoustic modes and mode conversion, extending material characterization beyond plane waves for acoustic metamaterials and liners.

  • 25 mm and 50 mm square waveguides
  • 8-microphone and 32-microphone configurations

Acoustic Liners and Fan Noise

Multimodal Acoustic Test Platform

An independently controlled loudspeaker array generates complex duct sound fields for measurements of modal propagation, conversion, transmission, and reflection.

  • Independent amplitude and phase control of 32 loudspeakers
  • Operating frequency range: 100–3500 Hz

Single-Stage Fan Test System

Measurements under fan operating conditions combine azimuthal mode decomposition with insertion-loss testing to evaluate duct noise and acoustic liner performance.

  • Duct diameter: 600 mm

Ducted Fan Test System

Tests in flowing ducts characterize acoustic transmission and liner noise reduction. Azimuthal and radial mode decomposition supports studies of modal propagation and attenuation.

  • Duct diameter: 150 mm
  • Operating frequency range: 1000–5000 Hz

Sound-Field and Vibration Measurements

Scanning Laser Doppler Vibrometry

Non-contact laser measurements map surface vibration for studies of structural response and vibration modes, complementing acoustic measurements.

Underwater Acoustic Field Scanning

Immersion transducers, hydrophones, and a two-axis positioning stage enable automated amplitude and phase mapping for studies of underwater transmission, scattering, and radiation.

  • Automated two-dimensional acoustic field scanning

Piezoelectric Sensor Array Testing

A piezoelectric sensor array and associated acquisition equipment complement the laboratory’s vibration and acoustic measurement platforms.

General Acoustic Instrumentation

  • XL2 Audio & Acoustic Analyzer: sound-level, spectral, and reverberation-time measurements.
  • DS3 Dodecahedron Speaker and PA3 Power Amplifier: sound excitation for room and building acoustics measurements.
  • NI data acquisition and signal generation equipment: multichannel measurements and analysis using in-house software.
