🔬 Experimental Facilities

The experimental facilities provide acoustic material testing, complex sound-field characterization, and noise and vibration measurements. Five platforms cover acoustic material testing, multimodal acoustic sources and characterization, fan acoustic mode characterization, underwater acoustic fields, and elastic wave fields, supported by general acoustic instrumentation.

1. Acoustic Material Testing Platform

The platform characterizes sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and sound transmission in materials and structures under conventional, high-sound-pressure, multimodal, and high-temperature conditions.

1.1 Conventional Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Measurements of normal-incidence sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and transmission loss.

Key Specifications

  • Circular sections: 29 mm diameter (500–6400 Hz) and 100 mm diameter (50–1600 Hz).
  • Square-section side lengths: 25, 30, 50, 70, 100, and 180 mm.
  • Combined frequency coverage of the square-section tube series: 20–6400 Hz.

1.2 High-Intensity Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Nonlinear acoustic characterization of absorbing materials and liners at high sound pressure levels.
  • Measurements of sound absorption and sound-pressure-level-dependent acoustic impedance.

Key Specifications

  • Sound pressure level range: 100–160 dB.
  • Operating frequency range: 50–3200 Hz.

1.3 Multimodal Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Higher-order acoustic mode decomposition and mode-conversion measurements.
  • Multimodal characterization of materials and structures within frequency bands where the relevant modes propagate.

Key Specifications

  • Square waveguides: 25 mm × 25 mm (8 microphones) and 50 mm × 50 mm (32 microphones).
  • Measurement frequency range: 50–12800 Hz; modal measurements are restricted to bands above the respective cutoff frequencies.

1.4 High-Temperature Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Measurements of material and structural sound absorption and acoustic impedance in heated gas.
  • Characterization of temperature-dependent acoustic properties.

Key Specifications

  • Maximum test temperature: 1000 °C.
  • Measurement frequency range: 50–6000 Hz.

2. Multimodal Acoustic Source and Characterization Platform

The platform provides multimodal duct sound-field generation, modal decomposition, and propagation measurements without flow for evaluating the acoustic performance of materials and structures.

Functions

  • Multimodal sound-field generation without flow, with controlled amplitude and phase.
  • Acoustic mode decomposition and mode-conversion measurements, with characterization of propagation, transmission, and reflection.

Key Specifications

  • Number of loudspeakers: 32.
  • Operating frequency range: 100–3500 Hz.

3. Fan Acoustic Mode Characterization Platform

The platform provides duct-noise measurements and acoustic mode decomposition under fan operating and flow conditions for evaluating fan-noise propagation and noise attenuation by acoustic materials.

3.1 Single-Stage Fan Test System

Functions

  • Duct-noise measurements under fan operating conditions.
  • Acoustic liner insertion-loss measurements for evaluating fan-noise attenuation.

Key Specifications

  • Duct diameter: 600 mm.
  • Fan rotational speed range: −3000 to 3000 rpm.
  • Acoustic measurement frequency range: 20–20000 Hz.

3.2 Ducted Fan Test System

Functions

  • Transmission-loss measurements of acoustic materials and liners in flowing ducts.
  • Azimuthal and radial mode decomposition for analyzing modal propagation and energy attenuation.

Key Specifications

  • Duct diameter: 150 mm.
  • Fan rotational speed range: 11000–15000 rpm.
  • Operating frequency range: 1000–5000 Hz.

4. Underwater Acoustic Field Characterization Platform

The platform characterizes spatial underwater acoustic fields and sound propagation through two-dimensional measurements of pressure amplitude and phase.

Functions

  • Underwater sound-field generation and spatial measurements of pressure amplitude and phase.
  • Experimental characterization of underwater acoustic transmission, scattering, and radiation.

Key Specifications

  • Scanning dimensionality: 2D.

5. Elastic Wave Field Characterization Platform

The platform characterizes structural vibration responses, spatial vibration distributions, and elastic-wave propagation using non-contact velocity and contact acceleration measurements.

5.1 Scanning Laser Doppler Vibrometry System

Functions

  • Non-contact measurements of the surface velocity component along the laser measurement direction.
  • Two-dimensional spatial vibration measurements for structural modal and elastic-wave characterization.

Key Specifications

  • Vibrometer frequency range: 0.5 Hz–22 kHz.
  • Two-axis scanning travel: approximately 1200 mm × 500 mm.

5.2 Piezoelectric Sensor Array Test System

Functions

  • Structural vibration acceleration measurements at individual or multiple locations.
  • Single-axis and triaxial measurements for structural modal and elastic-wave experiments.

Key Specifications

  • Sensor inventory: 15 single-axis and 7 triaxial accelerometers.
  • Single-axis sensor frequency ranges: 0.3 Hz–6 kHz or 1 Hz–8 kHz, depending on the selected sensor.
  • Triaxial sensor frequency range: 1 Hz–13 kHz.

6. General Acoustic Instrumentation

General-purpose instruments provide sound-level, spectral, reverberation, and multichannel measurements in support of the experimental platforms.

6.1 Audio and Acoustic Analyzer

Functions

  • Sound-level, spectral, and reverberation-time measurements.
  • Acoustic environment and room-acoustic parameter characterization.

Key Specifications

  • Frequency weightings: A, C, and Z.
  • Spectral analysis: octave bands, one-third-octave bands, and FFT.
  • Reverberation-time measurements: octave bands from 63 Hz to 8 kHz.

6.2 Omnidirectional Sound Source and Power Amplifier

Functions

  • Acoustic excitation for room and building acoustics testing.
  • Test sound-field generation for reverberation-time and airborne sound-insulation measurements.

Key Specifications

  • Number of loudspeakers: 12.
  • Equalized source frequency range: 100 Hz–8 kHz.

6.3 Multichannel Data Acquisition and Signal Generation

Functions

  • Source signal output, dynamic response acquisition, and digitized recording.
  • Experimental control, multichannel signal processing, and data analysis.
