🔬 Experimental Facilities

The experimental facilities comprise four platforms for acoustic material testing, multimodal noise generation and characterization, underwater acoustic field characterization, and elastic wave field characterization, supported by general acoustic instrumentation.

1. Acoustic Material Testing Platform

This platform integrates conventional, high-intensity, multimodal, and high-temperature impedance tube systems for characterizing sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and transmission under different excitation levels, modal conditions, and temperatures.

Conventional Impedance Tube System

Circular- and square-section impedance tubes, microphone arrays, and data acquisition equipment provide transfer-function measurements of normal-incidence sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and transmission loss. The usable frequency range depends on tube dimensions and measurement configuration.

  • Circular-section diameters: 29 and 100 mm
  • Square-section side lengths: 25, 30, 50, 70, 100, and 180 mm
  • Combined frequency coverage of the square-section tube series: 20–6400 Hz

High-Intensity Impedance Tube System

Multiple sound sources with horn coupling provide large-amplitude excitation for nonlinear acoustic testing of absorbing materials and liners. Measurements characterize absorption and sound-pressure-level-dependent impedance; tube sections are selected according to sample dimensions and target frequency range.

Multimodal Impedance Tube System

Square waveguides, microphone arrays, and multichannel acquisition equipment support higher-order acoustic mode decomposition and mode-conversion measurements. Material and liner responses are characterized within the applicable frequency bands above the relevant modal cutoff frequencies.

  • 25 mm × 25 mm waveguide: 8 microphones
  • 50 mm × 50 mm waveguide: 32 microphones

High-Temperature Impedance Tube System

A sound source, heater, microphone array, and water-cooled section provide measurements of sound absorption and acoustic impedance in heated gas. Controlled test temperatures enable characterization of temperature-dependent material and structural responses.

2. Multimodal Noise Generation and Characterization System

This system integrates a static multimodal acoustic source and single-stage and ducted fan test rigs for duct sound-field generation, modal decomposition, and acoustic liner characterization with and without flow.

Static Multimodal Acoustic Source System

An annular loudspeaker array, microphone arrays, and multichannel signal generation and acquisition equipment produce multimodal and vortex sound fields through independent amplitude and phase control. Measurements resolve acoustic modes and characterize propagation, conversion, transmission, and reflection without flow.

  • Independent amplitude and phase control of 32 loudspeakers
  • Operating frequency range: 100–3500 Hz

Single-Stage Fan Test System

A single-stage fan, test duct, microphone arrays, and acquisition equipment provide duct-noise measurements and azimuthal mode decomposition under fan operating conditions. Acoustic liner insertion-loss measurements quantify fan-noise attenuation.

  • Duct diameter: 600 mm
  • Frequency range for dominant azimuthal mode analysis: 200–1600 Hz

Ducted Fan Test System

A ducted fan, liner test section, and microphone arrays provide transmission-loss measurements and azimuthal and radial mode decomposition in flowing ducts. Modal and energy-attenuation analyses characterize acoustic transmission and liner noise-reduction performance.

  • Duct diameter: 150 mm
  • Operating frequency range: 1000–5000 Hz

3. Underwater Acoustic Field Characterization Platform

This platform provides underwater sound-field generation, two-dimensional scanning, and spatial measurements of acoustic pressure amplitude, phase, and propagation characteristics.

Underwater Acoustic Field Scanning System

Immersion transducers, hydrophones, a water tank, a two-axis positioning stage, and signal generation, amplification, and acquisition equipment provide automated sound-field scanning. Pressure amplitude and phase maps characterize underwater acoustic transmission, scattering, and radiation.

  • Automated two-axis positioning and sound-field scanning
  • Spatial acoustic pressure amplitude and phase measurements

4. Elastic Wave Field Characterization Platform

This platform combines non-contact laser vibrometry with piezoelectric acceleration measurements for structural vibration, elastic-wave propagation, and spatial vibration-field characterization.

Scanning Laser Doppler Vibrometry System

A laser Doppler vibrometer, two-axis positioning stage, motion controller, and synchronized acquisition equipment measure the surface velocity component along the laser measurement direction. Point-by-point scans map spatial vibration responses for elastic-wave and modal characterization without contact sensors at the measurement locations.

  • Vibrometer frequency range: 0.5 Hz–22 kHz
  • Two-axis scanning travel: approximately 1200 mm × 500 mm

Piezoelectric Sensor Array Test System

Single-axis and triaxial piezoelectric accelerometers with associated acquisition equipment measure vibration acceleration at individual or multiple locations. The system supports structural modal and elastic-wave experiments; measurement directions, frequency range, and dynamic range depend on sensor and acquisition configuration.

  • Single-axis and triaxial acceleration measurements
  • Single-point or multipoint vibration response acquisition

General Acoustic Instrumentation

General-purpose instruments provide sound-level, spectral, reverberation, and multichannel measurements in support of the experimental platforms.

Audio and Acoustic Analyzer

Integrated sound-level measurement, octave- and one-third-octave-band analysis, FFT spectral analysis, and reverberation-time measurement support acoustic environment and room-acoustic characterization.

Omnidirectional Sound Source and Power Amplifier

A dodecahedron loudspeaker and associated power amplifier provide acoustic excitation for room and building acoustics testing, including reverberation-time and airborne sound-insulation measurements.

Multichannel Data Acquisition and Signal Generation

Signal generation, dynamic signal acquisition, and digitizer modules provide source excitation and response recording. Associated software supports experimental control, data processing, and analysis; channel capacity and frequency range are determined by the test configuration.
