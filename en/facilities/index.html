🔬 Experimental Facilities

The experimental facilities provide acoustic material testing, complex sound-field characterization, and noise and vibration measurements. Four platforms cover acoustic materials, multimodal noise, underwater acoustic fields, and elastic wave fields, supported by general acoustic instrumentation.

1. Acoustic Material Testing Platform

Conventional, high-intensity, multimodal, and high-temperature impedance tube systems characterize material and structural sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and transmission under different excitation levels, modal conditions, and temperatures.

1. Conventional Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Transfer-function measurements of normal-incidence sound absorption, acoustic impedance, and transmission loss.
  • Complementary tube sizes cover different frequency bands; the usable range depends on tube dimensions and measurement configuration.

Key Specifications

  • Circular sections: 29 mm diameter (500–6400 Hz) and 100 mm diameter (50–1600 Hz).
  • Square-section side lengths: 25, 30, 50, 70, 100, and 180 mm.
  • Combined frequency coverage of the square-section tube series: 20–6400 Hz.
  • Measurement configuration: microphone arrays, data acquisition equipment, and associated software.

2. High-Intensity Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Large-amplitude acoustic excitation for nonlinear testing of absorbing materials and liners.
  • Measurements of sound absorption and sound-pressure-level-dependent acoustic impedance.

Key Specifications

  • Sound pressure level range: 100–160 dB.
  • Operating frequency range: 50–3200 Hz.

3. Multimodal Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Higher-order acoustic mode decomposition and mode-conversion measurements.
  • Multimodal characterization of materials and structures within frequency bands where the relevant modes propagate.

Key Specifications

  • Square waveguides: 25 mm × 25 mm (8 microphones) and 50 mm × 50 mm (32 microphones).
  • Measurement frequency range: 50–12800 Hz; modal measurements are restricted to bands above the respective cutoff frequencies.
  • Acquisition configuration: multichannel dynamic signal acquisition and associated modal analysis software.

4. High-Temperature Impedance Tube System

Functions

  • Measurements of material and structural sound absorption and acoustic impedance in heated gas.
  • Characterization of temperature-dependent acoustic responses through controlled test temperatures.

Key Specifications

  • Maximum test temperature: 1000 °C.
  • Measurement frequency range: 50–6000 Hz.
  • System components: sound source, heater, microphone array, and water-cooled section.
  • Temperature control: heating of the gas within the test tube.
  • Microphone protection: a water-cooled thermal protection section.

2. Multimodal Noise Generation and Characterization System

A static multimodal acoustic source and single-stage and ducted fan test rigs provide duct sound-field generation, modal decomposition, and noise-propagation measurements with and without flow, supporting material and structural evaluation in complex acoustic environments.

1. Static Multimodal Acoustic Source System

Functions

  • Controlled multimodal sound-field generation without flow through independent loudspeaker amplitude and phase control.
  • Microphone-array-based modal decomposition and measurements of propagation, conversion, transmission, and reflection.

Key Specifications

  • Source array: 32 loudspeakers with independent amplitude and phase control.
  • Operating frequency range: 100–3500 Hz.
  • Measurement configuration: microphone arrays and multichannel signal generation and acquisition equipment.

2. Single-Stage Fan Test System

Functions

  • Duct-noise measurements and dominant azimuthal mode decomposition under fan operating conditions.
  • Acoustic liner insertion-loss measurements for evaluating fan-noise attenuation.

Key Specifications

  • Duct diameter: 600 mm.
  • Fan rotational speed range: −3000 to 3000 rpm.
  • Acoustic measurement frequency range: 20–20000 Hz.
  • Frequency range for dominant azimuthal mode analysis: 200–1600 Hz.

3. Ducted Fan Test System

Functions

  • Transmission-loss measurements of acoustic materials and liners in flowing ducts.
  • Azimuthal and radial mode decomposition for analyzing modal propagation and energy attenuation.

Key Specifications

  • Duct diameter: 150 mm.
  • Fan rotational speed range: 11000–15000 rpm.
  • Operating frequency range: 1000–5000 Hz.

3. Underwater Acoustic Field Characterization Platform

Underwater sound sources, pressure measurement equipment, and two-axis scanning provide experimental characterization of spatial acoustic fields and sound propagation.

1. Underwater Acoustic Field Scanning System

Functions

  • Underwater sound-field generation and automated two-dimensional scanning of pressure amplitude and phase.
  • Experimental characterization of underwater acoustic transmission, scattering, and radiation.

Key Specifications

  • Positioning configuration: a two-axis stage and water tank.
  • Acoustic configuration: immersion transducers, a needle hydrophone, and a collision-resistant hydrophone.
  • Signal chain: signal generation, power amplification, and digitization equipment.

4. Elastic Wave Field Characterization Platform

Non-contact laser vibrometry and piezoelectric acceleration measurements characterize structural vibration responses, spatial vibration distributions, and elastic-wave propagation.

1. Scanning Laser Doppler Vibrometry System

Functions

  • Non-contact measurements of the surface velocity component along the laser measurement direction.
  • Two-dimensional point-by-point scanning of vibration responses for structural modal and elastic-wave characterization.

Key Specifications

  • Vibrometer frequency range: 0.5 Hz–22 kHz.
  • Two-axis scanning travel: approximately 1200 mm × 500 mm.
  • System configuration: laser vibrometer, positioning stage, motion control, and synchronized acquisition equipment.

2. Piezoelectric Sensor Array Test System

Functions

  • Structural vibration acceleration measurements at individual or multiple locations.
  • Single-axis and triaxial measurements for structural modal and elastic-wave experiments.

Key Specifications

  • Sensor inventory: 15 single-axis and 7 triaxial accelerometers.
  • Single-axis sensor frequency ranges: 0.3 Hz–6 kHz or 1 Hz–8 kHz, depending on the selected sensor.
  • Triaxial sensor frequency range: 1 Hz–13 kHz.
  • Measurement directions, dynamic range, and channel configuration depend on the sensors and acquisition equipment.

5. General Acoustic Instrumentation

General-purpose instruments provide sound-level, spectral, reverberation, and multichannel measurements in support of the experimental platforms.

1. Audio and Acoustic Analyzer

Functions

  • Sound-level, spectral, and reverberation-time measurements.
  • Acoustic environment and room-acoustic parameter characterization.

Key Specifications

  • Frequency weightings: A, C, and Z.
  • Spectral analysis: octave bands, one-third-octave bands, and FFT.
  • Reverberation-time measurements: octave bands from 63 Hz to 8 kHz.

2. Omnidirectional Sound Source and Power Amplifier

Functions

  • Acoustic excitation for room and building acoustics testing.
  • Sound-source configuration for reverberation-time and airborne sound-insulation measurements.

Key Specifications

  • Source configuration: 12 loudspeakers in a dodecahedral arrangement.
  • Equalized source frequency range: 100 Hz–8 kHz.
  • Associated amplifier: class-D power amplifier with pink-noise generation.

3. Multichannel Data Acquisition and Signal Generation

Functions

  • Source signal output, dynamic response acquisition, and digitized recording.
  • Experimental control, data processing, and analysis using associated software.

Key Specifications

  • Hardware configuration: modular acquisition platforms, dynamic signal acquisition modules, and high-speed digitizers.
  • Software configuration: experimental control, signal processing, and multichannel analysis.
  • Channel capacity and frequency range are determined by the configuration of each test system.
